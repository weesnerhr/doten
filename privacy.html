DoTEN

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

The short version: DoTEN does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your lists stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We can't see them.

What you put in the app

The tasks, lists, colors, links, and completion history you create in DoTEN are stored on your device. The DoTEN widget reads the same data from your device so it can show your list on your Home Screen.

iCloud sync

If you're signed into iCloud, DoTEN keeps your lists in sync across your iPhone and Mac using Apple's iCloud storage. That data is stored in your own iCloud account and handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't have access to it, and it is never sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. The app only asks Apple whether the purchase has been made so it can unlock the extra lists.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it's scheduled on your device by iOS. No reminder information leaves your device.

Links and phone numbers

If you add a link or phone number to a task and tap it, DoTEN opens it in your browser or phone app. Websites you visit have their own privacy policies.

Children

DoTEN doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Removing your data

You can delete any task or list inside the app, and the change syncs to your other devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove synced data from iCloud as well, delete your lists in the app before deleting it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email dotenapp@icloud.com.
